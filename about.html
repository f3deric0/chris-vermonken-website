The Company

Carrières du Hainaut

From Nature. From Here.

For more than 130 years, Carrières du Hainaut has extracted and manufactured this precious, unique, and authentic stone from the depths of our subsoil. Developments in expertise, know-how, and equipment have greatly contributed to the vigour and success of the company, now the largest ornamental limestone quarry in Europe.
